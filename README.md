# 🔗 NEXUS – Blockchain Intelligence & VASP Attribution Platform

### Smart India Hackathon 2026 | SIH26182

NEXUS is a blockchain intelligence platform designed to automate the attribution of unknown cryptocurrency wallets to their nearest Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs) through blockchain intelligence APIs.

The platform focuses on analyzing cryptocurrency transactions, identifying wallet relationships, tracing fund movements, and connecting unknown wallet addresses to potential VASPs or exchanges using graph-based intelligence and evidence-based attribution.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

NEXUS introduces an intelligent investigation workflow that transforms an unknown cryptocurrency wallet into an evidence-backed VASP/entity lead.

The proposed solution follows five major stages:

1. **Identify:** Collect unknown wallet addresses and transaction history.
2. **Trace:** Build transaction graphs and map fund movements.
3. **Cluster:** Identify wallet relationships and group related addresses.
4. **Attribute:** Link wallet clusters to potential VASPs or service providers.
5. **Prove:** Present supporting evidence and explain attribution results.

## ⚙️ Technical Methodology

The platform follows a six-stage technical process:

**1. Data Capture**
- Collect wallet addresses and transaction information.

**2. Normalization**
- Clean and structure wallet and transaction data.

**3. Clustering**
- Identify wallet relationships and group connected addresses.

**4. Path Tracing**
- Follow fund movements through transaction paths.

**5. Entity Attribution**
- Identify potential VASPs and cryptocurrency exchanges.

**6. Risk & Reporting**
- Generate attribution confidence scores and supporting evidence reports.
